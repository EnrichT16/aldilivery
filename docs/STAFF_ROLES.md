The people who run Ozi Delivery, and what each one sees

Written for Anthony, 7 October 2026. Each person has their own sign-in to the admin panel, at ozidelivery.co.uk/staff, and sees only the parts their job needs. The founder sees everything, and is the only one who can add people, change their job, give them a new password or turn their account off. The jobs are set in packages/api/src/lib/staff.ts; tell Claude to add or change one.


How to add someone

1. Sign in at /staff. Press "Founder: sign in with the staff key", and give your name and the STAFF_API_KEY.
2. Open the Team tab. Type their name, a username (for example ngozi.okafor), and choose their job. Press "Add to the team".
3. A first password is shown, once. Give it to them in person or by phone, never by email or chat.
4. The first time they sign in, they choose their own password. Nobody else, including you, ever knows it.
5. If they forget it, press "New password" beside their name. If they leave, press "Turn off": it works at once, even if they are signed in.

Every decision in the panel records the name of the person signed in, so it is always clear who decided what.

It is a good idea to make an account for yourself too, with the job Founder, so you do not need the staff key every day. Keep the key somewhere safe for emergencies.


The jobs, in the order to hire them

1. Customer care officer. Sees: Problems and Feedback.
   Reads every problem a Shopper or Runner reports, listens to the evidence, and decides it within two working days: who was at fault and what is refunded. Reads Runner feedback. The most important first hire: a kind, patient person with good judgement, ideally with experience of blind, partially sighted or elderly customers. Part time to start.

2. Runner onboarding officer. Sees: Documents.
   Checks each new Runner's right to work (with the share code, on gov.uk), their DBS certificate, driving licence and insurance, and accepts or turns them down. Needs to be careful and thorough: it is what keeps Shoppers safe. Part time; a few hours a week at first.

3. Finds It shopper. Sees: Finds It.
   Looks in up to three local shops for what a Shopper asked for, by phone or in person, and records what was found, where and the price, or that it was not found, which gives the fee back. Someone local who knows the shops. Can start as a few hours a day, or be one of your best Runners.

4. Partnerships officer. Sees: Enquiries.
   Rings back councils, charities, care providers and shops that ask to work with us, within two working days, and sets up organisation accounts and shop offers. Good on the phone, comfortable talking to managers. Part time.

5. Finance officer. Sees: Money owed.
   Keeps track of money Runners owe after a problem was their fault, writes off small amounts (£20 or less), and asks for larger ones. Later, payouts and the accounts. Could be your bookkeeper or accountant, a few hours a month.

6. Operations manager. Sees: Documents, Problems, Feedback, Finds It and Enquiries; not Money owed, and not the Team.
   Runs the day to day when you are not there, and covers for anyone away. Hire once there are three or four people, or when you want to step back from daily decisions.

7. Founder. Sees everything, including the Team. You, and anyone you make a co-founder or director.

At the very start, one or two people can do several jobs: for example, one person as Operations manager covers everything except money and the team.


Your own account, and people who see your dashboard (ruling 43)

Your owner's account is the only one that sees the money: what comes in from Stripe, and later Flutterwave, Paystack and any other gateway, and what goes back out in refunds. Not the staff key, not any staff, not family, not investors. You sign in to it with your username, password and your 7-character passcode, and a two-step code too if you turn those on.

Family (such as your wife) and investors get their own sign-in, from My settings, "Who sees my dashboard". Everything starts switched off. You switch on, one by one or all at once: the overview (how many Shoppers, Runners, Shop Partners, organisations and staff), business analysis, the team, Runner documents, complaints, feedback, Finds It, enquiries, and shops and organisations. The money is never in the list. They can look, but never change or decide anything.

The kill switch, in My settings, with your passcode: every switch for family and investors goes off, and everyone signed in to the admin panel is signed out, you included. Nothing is deleted. A fingerprint can be added to it later, in the phone apps.

Business analysis is for you, and for whoever you switch it on for.
