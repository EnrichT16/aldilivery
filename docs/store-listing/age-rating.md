# Age rating answers

Accounts are for people aged 18 and over (the privacy policy and terms), and no age-restricted
goods can be ordered in this version (`versionOneRestrictions.ageRestrictedGoodsAllowed` is
false in config/store.json). Nothing in the app is violent, frightening, sexual or about
gambling.

## Apple (App Store Connect, the app, App Information, Age Rating)

Apple's questionnaire was rewritten in 2025 and may change again; answer what it asks with
these facts.

- Cartoon or fantasy violence; realistic violence; prolonged graphic or sadistic violence;
  profanity or crude humour; mature or suggestive themes; horror or fear themes; sexual content
  or nudity; graphic sexual content: **None**.
- Alcohol, tobacco or drug use or references: **None**. (Alcohol cannot be ordered in this
  version. If that changes, this answer becomes "Infrequent", and the age rating should be 18+.)
- Simulated gambling; contests; real gambling (loot boxes): **None / No**.
- Medical or treatment information: **None**.
- Unrestricted web access: **No**. (The app opens only its own pages; links to maps open the
  maps app.)
- Messaging and chat, or user-generated content: **Yes**. Shoppers and Runners can speak on an
  in-app call and answer each other's questions about an item. Calls are not recorded; a person
  can report a problem, and staff can stop a Runner working.
- Advertising: **Yes** if the advert square or Spotlight is switched on when you submit,
  otherwise **No**.
- Parental controls; age assurance: **No**.

**Age rating to choose:** Apple will suggest a rating from the answers (likely 13+ because of
calls between people). Because accounts are for adults only, use the option to set a **higher
age rating** and choose **18+**. That keeps the app out of children's searches and matches the
terms. Check the option is still offered; if not, accept Apple's suggestion.

## Google (Play Console, App content, Content rating: the IARC questionnaire)

- Email for the rating certificate: hello@ozidelivery.co.uk (once it is a real mailbox), or
  Anthony's own.
- Category: **All other app types** (it is not a game, and not only a social or
  communication app).
- Violence, fear, sexuality, language, controlled substances, crude humour, gambling: **No** to
  each.
- Does the app allow users to interact or exchange content with each other? **Yes** (in-app
  calls between a Shopper and their Runner, and item questions).
- Does the app share the user's current physical location with other users? **No.** (A
  Runner's location goes only to our own staff during an SOS, never to a Shopper.)
- Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods? **Yes** while the Recipe Pass and Ozi
  Plus are on sale in the app; see docs/APP_STORES.md, "Before submitting", because that may
  change. Otherwise **No**.
- Does the app contain ads? Same answer as on the Data safety page.

Expected result: rated for everyone or teen in most countries. That is fine: the separate
**Target audience** answer (18 and over) is what keeps it out of children's sections.
