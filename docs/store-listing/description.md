## Full description (Apple 4,000; Google 4,000)

Anthony: the second paragraph mentions that you are registered blind. Take that sentence out
if you would rather the listing did not say it.

Ozi Delivery is grocery shopping you can do by talking. Tell Ozi what you need, in your own
words. Ozi reads your list back to you, tells you the price, and waits for you to say yes. A
local Runner then buys your shopping at your local supermarket and brings it to your door.

Made for blind and partially sighted people first

Ozi Delivery was designed by a founder who is registered blind, for people who find ordinary
shopping apps hard to use. Every screen works with VoiceOver and TalkBack. The text is large,
the buttons are big, and every icon has its words written underneath. You can pinch to zoom.
You never have to read a long screen: Ozi tells you what matters, and you can always tap
instead of talking.

It is just as useful if you are older, disabled, find reading hard, or simply cannot get to the
shop.

How it works

1. Say or tap what you need. Ozi reads it back to you.
2. You say yes. A Runner buys it at the shelf price, and you pay what the till receipt says.
3. It comes to your door. Delivery is £7.99 for a shop of £15 or less, £13.50 above, or £7.99
   with Ozi Membership and £5.99 with Ozi Plus. Every price you see includes a small item
   charge. No surge pricing, no small order fee and no minimum spend. Your first month of
   membership is free, and nothing is charged unless you choose to join.

Your Runner

Runners are local people whose right to work and DBS check we have checked. Every standard
delivery pays the Runner £5, whatever the size of your basket. Tell your Runner how to find
your door, such as "knock loudly, I am slow to the door". If something is not on the shelf,
your Runner asks you before choosing something else. You can ring your Runner with one big
green button, and neither of you ever sees the other's telephone number.

Following your order

A live page shows where your order has got to, and Ozi tells you out loud when it changes.
Notifications tell you when the app is closed.

If something goes wrong

Report a problem with a voice note or a photo, and a person decides within two working days.
Small refunds happen straight away.

For carers and families

A relative or carer can join a call to speak for you, and councils, charities and care
providers can arrange shopping for the people they look after.

Paying

Pay by card, through Stripe, or by bank transfer where it is offered. We never see your full
card number. Each order has a receipt you can download.

Your privacy

What you say to Ozi is never recorded or kept. We do not use advertising trackers. Your Runner
sees your name and address only while they have your order. You can close your account at any
time in Settings.

Where we are

We are starting in Gillingham, Kent, in the ME7 area, and growing from there. You can also
order by telephone.

Ozi Delivery is an independent service. We are not part of, or connected with, the
supermarkets our Runners shop at.

Accounts are for people aged 18 and over.
