## Sizes each store needs

- **Apple**: iPhone screenshots only, because the app is set to iPhone only (iPads run it as
  an iPhone app, and Apple then asks for no iPad screenshots). One set at the **6.9 inch**
  size: 1320 by 2868 pixels (an iPhone 16 Pro Max or 17 Pro Max) or 1290 by 2796 (iPhone 15 Pro
  Max). At least 1, up to 10. Apple shrinks them for smaller iPhones.
- **Google**: **phone** screenshots, at least 2 and up to 8, each side between 320 and 3,840
  pixels, upright, 9 by 16 is safest (for example 1080 by 1920). Any recent Android phone's own
  screenshot is fine.
- Taking one: on an iPhone, press the side button and volume up together. On most Android
  phones, power and volume down together.
